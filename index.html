Peer-Reviewed Publication

IEEE EPREC-2026

Hybrid Optimization for Congestion Management in Deregulated Power Systems

6th IEEE International Conference on Electric Power and Renewable Energy (EPREC-2026), IIT Bhilai • Published by IEEE

Addresses transmission line congestion in open-access deregulated grids via Power Transfer Distribution Factor (PTDF) sensitivity modeling. Introduces an adaptive tri-hybrid metaheuristic (coupling Orcas, Krill Herd, and Spotted Hyena algorithms) to redispatch generators and eliminate thermal overloads on IEEE 14-bus and 30-bus test systems while minimizing rescheduling cost.
