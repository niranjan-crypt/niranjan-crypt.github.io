KinoSync: Cyber–Physical Smart Glove & 3D Digital Twin

ESP32 · 5-Channel Flex Sensors · MPU6050 6-DoF IMU · MAX30102 · BLE 5.0

Real-Time 3D Hand Telemetry in WebGL · Bio-Sensing (SpO2 & Heart Rate)

Cyber-physical wearable glove capturing 14 joint angles via custom resistive flex sensors, hand orientation via 6-DoF IMU, and vital signs via photoplethysmography. Streams low-latency telemetry over BLE 5.0 to a cross-platform 3D digital twin visualization engine.
